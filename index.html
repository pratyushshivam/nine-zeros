What is real, and what is a model

  • The model. Machine counts come from the assumptions (how many requests a user makes, how many a server can serve). They are standard rules of thumb, listed and editable under Assumptions.
  • The slowdown. Response times under load use the M/M/1 queue: mean time is the service time divided by one minus the load. That is exact for that queue and is why a system at 95 percent is not “nearly fine”.
  • The prices are AWS public list prices, linked under Assumptions.
  • The companies on the rail are real, placed where their own engineers said they were, each with its source. They often did it with far fewer machines than the rules of thumb suggest. That is the point: architecture and care matter more than the rules.
